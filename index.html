What this is

We went through Pura Mente as a new user, reached your paywall, and checked it against the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and every year we publish what we see across them in State of Paywalls. We pointed that at Pura Mente: installed it, answered the onboarding, read your App Store listing, and compared your first paywall and what happens when someone closes it with what the report found.

What follows is four ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype of your paywall, in your design, with a switch to compare it against today.

Benchmarks are for Health & Fitness apps where the report breaks them out. Your biggest App Store market is Latin America, but much of your revenue comes from Google Play and the report covers the App Store only, so we compare how your paywall is set up and lean on A/B test results rather than conversion or price benchmarks.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your navy, your green button, your Hoy / En 5 días / En 7 días timeline, your 50% OFF offer. We moved things you already have to the moment people decide.

You may have tested some of this

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes before cosmetics, as the report recommends. Each one links straight to the screen in the prototype.

01

Show your 50% offer when someone closes the first paywall

Today the X on your onboarding paywall opens “¿Estás seguro?” and then the app. Your own offer, 50% OFF at 17,99 € a year, already exists, but it only shows up if someone later opens and closes the paywall in Profile.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps show a paywall after the first is closed. It converts at 0.88× the main one.
See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
02

Put monthly next to the annual trial

The first paywall sells one plan: 33,83 € a year after 7 free days. Your 6,63 € monthly plan only appears later, in Profile. Side by side, the annual reads as 2,82 € a month, less than half. Your own “Sin compromisos, cancela cuando quieras” goes under the button.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Changing the number of plans wins 57% of A/B tests on LTV; visual-only changes win 35%.
See the screen CR +15–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
03

Put your 4,8 stars and 35,000 people on the paywall

Two screens before the paywall you say “+35,000 personas eligen Pura Mente”, and your Spanish App Store listing shows 4,8 from 8,1 mil ratings. Neither is on the screen where people decide.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof.
See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
04

Use the goal they picked in the headline

Your timeline already says “Cultiva tu amor propio” to someone who chose self-esteem. The headline above it still says “Inicia tu semana gratis” to everyone. Put the goal at the top, e.g. “Empieza gratis tu semana de amor propio”.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 2% of top paywall headlines use what the app knows about the user.
